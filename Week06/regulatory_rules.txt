DERIVATIVE REPORTING

Derivative trades require a Unique Transaction Identifier (UTI).
The UTI should uniquely identify the reported transaction.

EQUITY REPORTING

Equity trades require a valid Legal Entity Identifier (LEI).
The LEI identifies the legal entity participating in the transaction.

TRADE VALIDATION

Trade amounts must be greater than zero.
Trades with missing mandatory identifiers should fail validation.

COMPLIANCE TRAINING

Employees involved in regulated activities must complete annual compliance training.
Training completion should be recorded for audit purposes.
